Formats

PDF, DOCX, EPUB, and text files (TXT, MD, JSON, CSV, code). Old .doc and .mobi files are not supported: save them as DOCX or EPUB first.

Tips

Open the working in one panel and the answer key in the other. Each panel has its own page box, zoom, full screen and minimize. Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms. Drop a file onto a panel to open it. Press F for full screen. For DOCX and EPUB, the page box counts screens, and the Contents button jumps by heading or chapter.

Privacy

Everything runs inside this one file. No network, no uploads, nothing is stored except your theme choices.
